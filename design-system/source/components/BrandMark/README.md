The BrandMark is the header lockup and links to "/". It combines the 22px Y logo, the mono `wordmark` (`YORKSTEAD` in `foreground` and ` SYSTEMS` in `primary`) and, from xl, the `descriptor` "Tailored business software and workflow systems" after a `border` rule.

**The consumer provides** `showDescriptor` (default `true`) and `className`. `BrandLogo` alone takes `size` (default 22).

**Don't** retype the wordmark in another face, change its 0.2em tracking, or swap the logo for another export than `/brand/logo/1x/logo-light.png`.
