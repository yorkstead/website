## Risks and design decisions

- Use the persisted inquiry row as the business conversion authority. Add a nullable foreign key from new server-generated outcome events to `contact_inquiries`; clients cannot set it. Do not backfill old events or reinterpret legacy `lead` rows. Report measured/linkable and legacy/unlinked outcomes separately where needed.
- Keep CTA event counts separate from inquiries. Use unique inquiry IDs for inquiries and sessions for traffic, with comparisons computed over equal adjacent windows.
- Remove restaurant and other form-value properties from analytics. Keep business conversion metadata to fixed non-sensitive categories only.
- Enforce body limits while reading bytes, normalize path and attribution server-side, and reject requests without a valid same-origin browser context. Trust address headers only according to the deployment's documented Vercel proxy contract; do not build a public user-controlled identity.
- Use stable per-navigation event keys for page views and retain unique IDs per intentional CTA click. Server-generated inquiry outcomes also carry the inquiry ID, permitting DB uniqueness/idempotency without guessing from personal data.
- Avoid a second campaign source of truth: add only an explicit mapping on existing marketing campaign/content records if a stable record is confirmed. If no campaign identity is maintainable, display normalized UTM campaign strings as observed labels and explain they are unverified rather than joining them to prospects or ROI.
